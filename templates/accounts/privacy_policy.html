At Virtus, your privacy and financial security are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains transparently how we collect, handle, and safeguard your information when you browse our catalog, create an account, or purchase digital products.

01. Information We Collect

We only collect the essential information required to fulfill your orders and manage your account:

  • Account Details: When you register an account, we store your chosen username, email address, first name, last name, and securely hashed passwords.
  • Order & Delivery Email: When checking out as a guest or member, we collect your email to transmit your instant product download links, license keys, and itemized receipts.
  • Transaction Records: We maintain order metadata including purchased items, total purchase amount, timestamp, currency (PHP), and confirmed payment status.

02. Payment Security & Encryption

{% lucide "shield-alert" size=18 class="guarantee-icon" %} Our Core Security Guarantee

Virtus never handles, processes, or stores your raw credit/debit card numbers or bank login PINs. All financial transactions are processed securely via PayMongo through BSP-regulated payment networks with 256-bit SSL bank-grade encryption.

Our payment infrastructure guarantees your safety across all supported payment rails:

  • QR Ph & E-Wallets: Dynamic QR Ph codes are generated through official Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) payment rails via PayMongo. You pay directly inside your familiar app (GCash, Maya, BPI, BDO, UnionBank, GoTyme, SeaBank, and 40+ banks) without sharing financial details with us.
  • Card Payments & Tokenization: Card details are tokenized directly in your browser using secure bank encryption before reaching our servers.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (3D Secure): Card purchases undergo direct verification with your card issuer via OTP or biometric approval.

03. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate commerce purposes:

  • Deliver digital files, license keys, and templates immediately upon payment confirmation.
  • Send official purchase receipts and payment confirmations to your inbox.
  • Maintain your active cart and provide order history in your member profile.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and secure the marketplace for all buyers.
  • Provide responsive customer support for any fulfillment or licensing questions.

04. Service Providers & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only partner with verified, enterprise-grade service providers under strict data privacy agreements:

  • Payment Partner: PayMongo Philippines, Inc. (BSP-licensed payment operator) for transaction routing, settlement, and fraud prevention.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: High-security cloud hosting with automated firewall protection, SSL/TLS encryption, and continuous monitoring.

05. Cookies & Session Storage

We use essential cookies and local session data strictly to enable platform functionality:

  • Session Management: Preserves your shopping cart items as you browse products and keeps you signed in securely.
  • Security Tokens: Protects your account and checkout forms against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF).

We do not use intrusive third-party tracking or behavioral ad networks.

06. Data Security & Retention

We enforce multi-layered defense protocols: 256-bit TLS encryption in transit, modern password hashing, parameterized database queries, and role-based access limits.

We retain order records for as long as necessary to provide lifetime access to your purchased digital goods and comply with accounting and legal standards.

07. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and global standards, you have the right to:

  • Access and download the personal records associated with your account.
  • Update or correct your account details anytime in your Profile Dashboard.
  • Manage your password and security credentials via Account Settings.
  • Request the closure and deletion of your account and personal records by contacting support.
